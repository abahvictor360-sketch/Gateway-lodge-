Privacy Policy

Gateway Lodge Group is committed to protecting the privacy of our guests, website visitors and partners. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as when you make a reservation, submit a contact or enquiry form, or subscribe to updates from The Gateway Journal. This may include your name, email address, phone number, and details relevant to your booking or enquiry.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to process reservations and enquiries, respond to guest requests, improve our properties and services, and communicate offers relevant to you, where you have consented to receive them.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell guest information. We may share information with trusted service providers who support our booking and reservation systems, strictly for the purpose of fulfilling your request.

Your Choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting us at info@gatewaylodgegroup.com.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to our team via our Contact page.
