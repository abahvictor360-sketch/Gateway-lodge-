Consistent Comfort

One Standard, Every Room

Whichever Gateway Lodge property you choose, you can expect clean, comfortable, well-maintained accommodation and attentive service from our team.

Nineteen Rooms, Three Destinations

One Group, Three Kinds of Stay

Gateway Lodge Group currently operates nineteen rooms across three Ghanaian properties. That is deliberately modest, and it is the point: at this scale the team knows who is staying, what they asked for and when they are arriving. Each property answers a different need, so the right choice depends less on price than on why you are travelling.

Choosing Well

Which Property Suits Your Trip?

  • Travelling for business in Accra, and want to be central: Nova Ridge
  • Travelling with family, or staying more than a few nights: Lakeside
  • Want a pool and a restaurant on site: Lakeside
  • Working in or travelling through Northern Ghana: Tamale
  • Booking for a group and need several rooms together: Lakeside or Tamale
  • Want the whole property to yourself: Nova Ridge
