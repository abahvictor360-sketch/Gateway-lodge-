The Lodge

The largest of the three, and the calmest.

Lakeside sits east of the centre in the Lakeside Estate, far enough out that the evenings are quiet and close enough that the city is still a drive rather than a journey. Twelve rooms and units, a front desk that is staffed around the clock, and grounds you can actually walk in.

It is the property guests choose when they are travelling as a family, when a team needs several rooms under one roof, or when the point of the trip is to slow down for a few days.

Room for a group

Twelve units means a family, a team or a small event fits in one place.

Genuinely quiet

Residential surroundings and grounds, rather than a main-road frontage.

Full service

A staffed front desk, dining on site and housekeeping every day.
