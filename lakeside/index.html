The Lodge

The largest of the three, and the calmest.

Lakeside sits east of the centre in the Lakeside Estate, far enough out that the evenings are quiet and close enough that the city is still a drive rather than a journey. Twelve serviced apartments in one gated block, each with its own kitchen, laundry and living room.

It is the property guests choose when they are travelling as a family, when a team needs several apartments under one roof, or when the point of the trip is to slow down for a few days.

Room for a group

Twelve apartments means a family, a team or a small event fits in one place.

Genuinely quiet

A gated residential estate, rather than a main-road frontage.

Properly self-contained

A full kitchen and laundry in every apartment, not a kitchenette.
