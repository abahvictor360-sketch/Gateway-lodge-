About Us

An Expanding Group, Built on Genuine Hospitality

Who We Are

We're More Than a Collection of Properties. We're a Growing Hospitality Group.

Gateway Lodge Group operates a portfolio of distinct hospitality destinations across Ghana — Gateway Nova Ridge, Gateway Lodge Lakeside and Gateway Lodge Tamale — each with its own character, united by a single standard of quality, comfort and care.

We exist to give every guest a place that feels considered, wherever in Ghana their journey takes them, while building toward a future that includes residences, real estate and new hospitality destinations still to come.

Our Hospitality Philosophy

Hospitality First, Always

Every Gateway Lodge property is run on the same principle: guests should feel welcomed, valued and cared for from arrival to departure. That means consistent quality across every room, attentive service from every team member, and a genuine warmth that reflects the best of Ghanaian hospitality.

We believe reliability and comfort aren't features — they're the baseline every guest deserves, at every one of our destinations.
