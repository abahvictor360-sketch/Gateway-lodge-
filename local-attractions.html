Where We Are

Two Regions, Two Very Different Days Out

Gateway Lodge Group operates in Accra and in Tamale. Those are around six hundred kilometres and a genuine cultural distance apart, and the attractions near each property reflect that. What follows is orientation rather than an itinerary.

Around Accra

Nova Ridge and Lakeside

  • Central Accra's national memorials and monuments
  • The National Museum and the city's cultural institutions
  • Osu and the capital's restaurant and nightlife districts
  • Accra's beaches and coastal road
  • Markets and craft centres across the city
  • Botanical gardens and hill country within a day's drive
  • Kotoka International Airport, for arrivals and departures
Around Tamale

Northern Region

  • Mole National Park, Ghana's largest wildlife reserve
  • Historic northern mosque architecture
  • Tamale central market and the city's trading quarter
  • Traditional weaving, smock-making and leather craft
  • Regional cultural festivals through the year
  • Onward routes across the Northern Region
