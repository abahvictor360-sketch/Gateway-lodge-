Why This Offer

Why It Makes Sense

Nova Ridge, Lakeside and Tamale are genuinely different places. A poolside weekend means something at Lakeside that it cannot mean in the Ridge district, and a regional business rate matters more in Tamale. Property-specific offers exist for exactly that reason.

What You Get

Included With This Offer

Nova Ridge, Accra

Lakeside, Accra

Tamale, Northern Region

Six units geared to business and regional travel, with offers to match.
